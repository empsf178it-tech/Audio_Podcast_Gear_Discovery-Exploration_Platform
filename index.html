Acoustic Fundamentals

Why Sound Quality Changes

Audio quality is determined by three interconnected factors before signal processing occurs.

01. Room Environment

Untreated hard surfaces reflect vocal energy, causing boxy room echo that no software plugin can cleanly eradicate.

02. Pickup Pattern

Choosing between dynamic capsules and sensitive condenser diaphragms dictates off-axis noise isolation.

03. Accurate Monitoring

High-fidelity closed-back monitor headphones allow you to detect plosive distortion and hum before publishing.
